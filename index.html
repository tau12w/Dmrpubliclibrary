Our Story

A Library Serving Readers Since 1951

The North District Public Library, Dharmanagar, has been a beacon of knowledge and literacy for over seven decades. Established in 1951, it serves as the primary public knowledge centre for the people of North Tripura.

With 60,027+ volumes spanning Bengali, English, and other languages — including the rare 40-volume Encyclopaedia Britannica — the library is a treasured cultural institution for the region.

1951

Library Established

Founded in Dharmanagar, beginning its mission of serving the reading community of North Tripura.

2007

New Building Inaugurated

A modern new building was inaugurated on 15 October 2007, significantly expanding capacity and facilities.

Today

Growing Digital Presence

Taking the next step into the digital age to serve readers online and preserve its legacy for future generations.

Rules & Conduct

Library Guidelines

Helping everyone enjoy a peaceful and productive experience. Please follow these guidelines during your visit.

🤫

Maintain Silence

The reading hall is a quiet space. Please speak in low voices at all times to respect fellow readers.

📱

Phones on Silent

Keep your phone on silent or vibrate mode. Step outside to make or receive calls.

🆔

Carry Your Card

Members must carry their library card when borrowing books. Valid ID proof is required for visitors.

📅

Return Books on Time

Borrowed books must be returned within the stipulated period to allow other members access.

🤲

Handle Books with Care

Treat all books gently. Do not fold pages, write in margins, or damage the covers of any book.

🧹

Keep It Clean

Maintain cleanliness within the premises. Use dustbins and keep your reading area tidy.

🍔

No Food or Drinks

Food, snacks, and beverages are strictly not allowed inside the library to protect books and maintain hygiene.

🚫

No Misuse of Resources

Library computers are for educational use only. Misuse may result in suspension of membership.

📋

Borrowing Rules

Members may borrow books up to the value of their security deposit (₹200 / ₹300 / ₹500). A maximum of 2 books may be borrowed at a time. All borrowed materials must be returned in good condition. The library reserves the right to charge for lost or damaged books. Membership is non-transferable.

Pather Panchali

by Bibhutibhushan Bandyopadhyay

One of the most celebrated novels in Bengali literature, Pather Panchali (Song of the Little Road) is a moving story of childhood, poverty, and the beauty of rural Bengal.
